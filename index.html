Who We Are

A Bible-based, Christ-centered,
Spirit-filled church

We are a family of believers in Phoenix seeking to know God through His Word and to make Him known. Our name comes from the believers in Berea, who “received the word with all readiness of mind, and searched the scriptures daily” (Acts 17:11). That is who we strive to be — a people who love Scripture, examine everything by it, and let it shape how we live.

Our Mission

Impart the Word in a way that impacts the world.

Through weekly worship, the faithful teaching of God’s Word, prayer, discipleship, and outreach in our community, we exist to build believers who learn, love, lift, link, and lead — from our neighborhood to the greater Phoenix area and beyond.

Our Pastor

Pastor W. R. Thomas

Pastor Thomas leads Berean Community Fellowship with a heart for the faithful teaching of God’s Word and a deep love for the people of Phoenix.

What We Believe

Measured by Scripture

We hold to the historic Christian faith and stand on the authority of God’s Word. We are conservative in our convictions, aligning with Scripture above all.

✝

The Scriptures

The Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God — our final authority in all of life. Like the Bereans, we test every teaching against it.

✝

The Gospel

Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Jesus Christ alone — fully God and fully man, crucified, risen, and returning.

✝

The Spirit-filled Life

We believe in the person and work of the Holy Spirit, who indwells, empowers, and transforms every believer to live a holy life.

What to expect

Come as you are. There’s no dress code — some dress up, many come casual.

A warm welcome. Look for a greeter near the entrance; they’ll help you find your way.

Worship & the Word. Expect worship, prayer, and a message from God’s Word — about 90 minutes together.

Families welcome. Children and families are always welcome at Berean.
