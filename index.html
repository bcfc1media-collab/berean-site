Our Story

Imparting the Word to impact the world

From a vision shared around a restaurant table to a thriving fellowship in Phoenix — the story of Berean is a testimony of God’s sustaining grace.

Berean began in 2001 with a burden and a vision. God laid on the heart of Dr. Fredrick L. Fairley Sr. a desire to reach a new generation with the unchanging message of Jesus Christ. Having served in ministry in Phoenix, he recognized the challenge of reaching succeeding generations — and became convinced of a simple truth: the message of the gospel must never change, but the methods we use to carry it must be willing to meet a changing world.

On October 27, 2001, Pastor Fairley gathered a group of believers around a table at a Denny’s near 51st Avenue and Indian School Road and shared the vision for a new church — committed to biblical teaching, evangelism, spiritual growth, and effective ministry. Inspired by Acts 17:10–11, they took the name Berean, after the believers who eagerly received God’s Word and searched the Scriptures daily to see whether what they were taught was true.

“The Bible is our middle name.”Berean’s 10th-anniversary theme

The vision caught fire. Berean held its first worship service on November 4, 2001, with more than 150 people present — and within that first month, over 140 people united with the new congregation. On December 2, 2001, the church held its formal organizational service, with messages brought by Bishop Alexis A. Thomas of Pilgrim Rest Baptist Church and Pastor Thomas E. Fisher, joined by pastors and supporters from across the Valley.

What began around a table grew into a flourishing ministry. Over the following years, Berean developed ministries for youth, music, Christian education, mentoring, and more, and hosted Bible conferences devoted to the faithful exposition of God’s Word. By its tenth anniversary, the congregation had grown to more than 500 — and in 2011 embraced the name Berean Bible Church, reinforcing a lifelong commitment to the authority, study, and proclamation of Scripture under the theme that still drives us: imparting the Word to impact the world.

Like every family, Berean has known seasons of triumph and seasons of testing. Through financial trials, changes of location, and a transition in leadership, one truth held firm: a church is not its walls. Through every season, the people of Berean kept gathering for worship, kept serving one another, and kept carrying the Word forward — a reminder that the identity of a church is never confined to any one building.

Today, Berean is led by Senior Pastor W. R. Thomas — a minister raised up and ordained under Berean’s founding pastor, who returned to shepherd the congregation through a season of transition and was called to lead it forward. In 2023, following that transition, the congregation was established as a new nonprofit, Berean Community Fellowship — a new organizational chapter in a story that reaches back to 2001. The name has grown, but the mission remains the same.

We remember with gratitude the founding pastor, the deacons and ministry leaders, the teachers, musicians, and families whose faithful service built and sustained this church. As the Apostle Paul wrote, one plants, another waters, but God gives the increase (1 Corinthians 3:6). To God be the glory for all He has done.

What We Believe

Measured by Scripture

These are the cornerstones of our faith. We hold to the historic Christian faith, stand on the authority of God’s Word, and are conservative in our convictions — aligning with Scripture above all.

The Scriptures
The Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God — our final authority in all matters of faith and life. Like the Bereans, we test every teaching against the Scriptures.2 Timothy 3:16–17 · Acts 17:11
God
We believe in one eternal God, existing in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.Matthew 28:19
Jesus Christ
Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man — born of a virgin, crucified for our sins, risen from the dead, and coming again.John 1:1,14 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4
The Holy Spirit
The Holy Spirit indwells, empowers, and transforms every believer, producing holy living and equipping the church with spiritual gifts.John 14:16–17 · Galatians 5:22–23
Salvation
Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Jesus Christ alone — a gift of God, not earned by works.Ephesians 2:8–9
The Church
The church is the body of Christ, called to worship God, make disciples, and serve the world in love.Acts 2:42–47
Baptism & the Lord’s Supper
We observe believer’s baptism by immersion and the Lord’s Supper as ordinances given by Christ to His church.Matthew 28:19 · 1 Corinthians 11:23–26
Marriage, Family & Sexuality
We believe marriage is the covenant union of one man and one woman, and that God’s design for sexuality and family is good and life-giving.Genesis 2:24 · Matthew 19:4–6
The Sanctity of Life
We believe every human life bears God’s image and is sacred from conception to natural death.Psalm 139:13–16
Last Things
We believe in the personal return of Jesus Christ, the resurrection of the dead, and life everlasting with God.1 Thessalonians 4:16–17 · Revelation 21:1–4
