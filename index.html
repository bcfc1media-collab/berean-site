Who We Are

A Bible-based, Christ-centered,
Spirit-filled church

We are a family of believers in Phoenix seeking to know God through His Word and to make Him known. Our name comes from the believers in Berea, who “received the word with all readiness of mind, and searched the scriptures daily” (Acts 17:11). That is who we strive to be — a people who love Scripture, examine everything by it, and let it shape how we live.

Our Story

How Berean came to be

Berean Community Fellowship began in 2001, when Pastor Fredrick L. Fairley became burdened for reaching a new generation with the unchanging message of Jesus Christ. He became convicted of a simple truth: the message must never change, but the methods must, in order to reach people for Christ.

On Saturday, October 27, 2001, Pastor Fairley gathered a group of believers at a Denny’s restaurant at the corner of 51st Avenue and Indian School Road and shared a vision for a new church that would meet the challenges of a new century. God’s peace was present and His purpose for His people was revealed — and, as on the day of Pentecost, the people were all in one place and of one accord. The church’s Organizational Service was held on December 2, 2001, with messages brought by Bishop Alexis A. Thomas of Pilgrim Rest Baptist Church and Pastor Thomas E. Fisher.

In 2023, Berean Community Fellowship was established through the shared vision, faith, and commitment of a dedicated group of believers who, recognizing the need for a new organizational structure, sought to preserve their identity as Bereans. United by their devotion to God, their commitment to biblical principles, and their desire to continue the work of ministry, these founding members came together to build a fellowship rooted in faith, unity, spiritual growth, and service to the community.

What We Believe

Measured by Scripture

We hold to the historic Christian faith and stand on the authority of God’s Word. We are conservative in our convictions, aligning with Scripture above all.

✝

The Scriptures

The Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God — our final authority in all of life. Like the Bereans, we test every teaching against it.

✝

The Gospel

Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Jesus Christ alone — fully God and fully man, crucified, risen, and returning.

✝

The Spirit-filled Life

We believe in the person and work of the Holy Spirit, who indwells, empowers, and transforms every believer to live a holy life.
