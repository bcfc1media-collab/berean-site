About Berean

Who we are

A Bible-based, Christ-centered, Spirit-filled church rooted in the Word and reaching the world.

Our Mission

Rooted in the Word, reaching the world

Our name comes from the believers in Berea, who “received the word with all readiness of mind, and searched the scriptures daily” (Acts 17:11). That is who we strive to be — a people who love Scripture, examine everything by it, and let it shape how we live and love. We exist to impart the Word in a way that impacts the world.
