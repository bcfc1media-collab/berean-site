What to expect

Come as you are. There’s no dress code — some dress up, many come casual. Just come as you are.

A warm welcome. Look for a greeter near the entrance; they’ll help you find your way and answer any questions.

Worship & the Word. Expect heartfelt worship, prayer, and a message from God’s Word — about 90 minutes together.

Families welcome. Children and families are always welcome at Berean.
